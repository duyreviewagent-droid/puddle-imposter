HOW TO PLAY

💧 WATER

Walk to the yellow rings and do your tasks. When all the Water finishes, Water wins.

Find a body? REPORT it. Saw something? Press the red emergency button in the middle room.

In meetings, accuse, defend and vote. The puddle with the most votes jumps into the lava.

Fix sabotage: flip the breakers when the lights go out, and hold both coolant valves during a heatwave.

🔥 FIRE

Set the Water on fire without being seen. Burn someone when nobody is watching, then slip into a vent.

Sabotage the lights so they can't see, or start a heatwave that melts everyone if it isn't fixed in time.

Fake tasks, blame someone else, and win when Fire equals Water.

SPECIAL ROLES

🚽 Toilet (Water) — stand on any vent, press F, flush to any other vent.

🌧️ Raining (Water) — press F anywhere to wash away the lights/heatwave emergency.

🧯 Fire Extinguisher (Water) — press F by a burned body to revive it (2 per game, only before it's reported). You can smell smoke on the minimap.

☁️ Evaporation (Water) — press F to float out of your body as vapor for 20 s, through walls, seeing everything. F again to go back. Your body stays behind and can still be burned!

🦄 Unicorn (Water) — F rides a rainbow into the sky for 15 s: you see the whole map. F to slide down. If Fire burns the bottom of the rainbow, you fall and die.

🫧 Bubble (Water) — F next to someone sticks a tracker bubble on them (3 per game). They show on your map, and if they kill you get an alert.

🌊 Underwater (Water) — F floods the whole map for 15 s; nobody can be killed while it's underwater (2 per game, 40 s reload).

🧽 Sponge (Fire) — soaks puddles up. No body left behind.

🧊 Ice (Fire) — press F to freeze ALL the Water (3× per game, 30 s reload). Frozen puddles wait 2 s, then spam-click 12 times to break out.

🌋 Eruption (Fire) — F calls an asteroid on any Water puddle. They get a 2 s red warning circle to run. Start with 2, +1 every 45 s, up to 3.

🪣 Bucket (Fire) — scoops puddles into a bucket, then press F at one of the 4 big buckets to dump them for good. Slower while carrying.
